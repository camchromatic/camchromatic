The Color Theorist

Cam Garcia

I’m Cam, a 31-year-old south Florida-based Hispanic American creative mind. I like to draw characters doing things in sci-fi settings, but I spend most of my time making simple jewelry nowadays. Sometimes I play tabletop or video games. I enjoy color, psychology, and color theory.

I have recently made the switch from my passive hobbies to focusing on active hobbies. I hope you’ll find use in the tools I’ve made.

If you’d like to ask about a piece, a project, or potential collaboration, you can get in touch.
