Well, here we are. It's November 1st and I wiped my Instagram for, hopefully, the last time. In hindsight, I should've kept it up for proof that I've been an artist for some time. If you're reading this and have your doubts, please rest assured; drawing and sketching digitally and traditionally has been my hobby for many, many years. I just... got tired of it not going anywhere. Or, rather, I got tired of it not getting me anywhere.  

I decided to make my own website. Initially, I thought of just making it my portfolio. There was just one small (actually, big) problem. There was nothing worth showcasing. All I had were just sketchdumps. Every once in a while, a "finished piece" was just a polished grayscale sketch or something with very messy lines and color. I have always had high standards for myself when it comes to considering a piece "finished," but somehow kept allowing myself to make really... uh... "mid" art. Hey, as artists, we're our worst critics!  

Anyway, I'll keep this post short. I'm hoping to make some cool things (art or not) and post them here.
