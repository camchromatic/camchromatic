++**[HEXACHROMAGON++**](https://hexachromagon.com)  
Folks, that's a link! I'm very excited to announce that HEXACHROMAGON (previously *Project HEXA*) is close to open beta. I'll save the bulk of important details for release day, but I can give you some insight.

- It's an avatar forum community, with a game attached, all with a focus on art. Originally designed as an auto-battler, I've decided it would be best to ship it alongside a website. I grew up with girly pixel dress-up sites and eventually joined several popular online communities for avatar forums. I also spent a lot of time on certain art communities and other sites. I'm aware I'm aiming for a very specific group but I just haven't found anything similar to what I want. So, uh, I'm making it!
- The game is based around color theory. It's a sci-fi theme. You do **not** have to play the game for any reason, other than if you want some lore. It's free-to-play, there's no microtransactions, and all assets are human-made. If you do want games, there's minigames! Mostly puzzle-like, and they're also about color theory.
- Things you can do: Make your avatar, play the game (casual and story mode), play the minigames, engage with the community, make friends, draw on our custom in-browser art app *Chromapad*, and more!
- The NPCs in this game are characters I've had for almost 20 years. Some of them have never seen the light of social media, and so will be making their debut here. I'm happy I could find these guys a permanent and public home.

This is all new to me, by the way. I've wanted an art-centric community that WASN'T about getting likes or comments, and that also provides inspiration. There's daily palette challenges, contests, and you can submit your own items and ideas! I want this to be a collaborative effort among artists. 

There will be supporter perks. I'm working those out before I release anything, but they're mostly premium features for the art app and forum cosmetics. All supporters are "subscribed" and would receive monthly items, unlock tools and certain settings in the art app, and get forum/display perks. Again, nothing is tied to the game. More details to come closer to release date.

++**[recolor.me Profile Editor](https://camchromatic.com/recolor-profile-editor/)**++

That's also a link! This one's been out for a week or so, but it's something I've wanted to build for a while. I've been on [recolor.me](http://recolor.me) for over 10 years (I joined a few months into its release) and I used to make and maintain guides and all sorts of features on there, including my popular BBCode resource. I made a handful of profile/bio templates at some point, but as the site updated, the formatting got a little jumbled. 

This tool helps my fellow [recolor.me](http://recolor.me) users preview and edit their profiles' formatting to be at it's best! I personally prefer to have a non-scrolling page, but you can do whatever you want! 

++**Art Updates**++

I've been so busy with coding and fine-tuning HEXA that I haven't drawn much lately. But, if you remember, I mentioned I had my own custom tool for drawing on my browser to encourage me to draw as much as possible. The Chromapad feature on HEXA (yes I'm calling it that for now) is a toned down version of my personal art app. I placed it in HEXA in hopes it can help others with their art-posting goals. I'll be drawing some placeholder assets in that app, and I'll also continue to post doodles on here. Despite not using it as frequently as I'd like, I do feel much better about my art. The tool has had several upgrades, including layer opacity, smoothing and pixel options, pen-pressure sensitivity (unfortunately needs Windows Ink on because I have a Huion), and a better starting palette for my personal and professional work.
